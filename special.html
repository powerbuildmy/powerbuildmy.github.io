How It Works – Molecular Metal Treatment

Power Build is NOT an oil additive. It is a permanent molecular treatment. Originally developed for military use and extreme environments, our positively charged molecules are magnetically attracted to the negatively charged metal surface inside your engine. Under heat and pressure, they bond permanently, penetrating up to 0.5 micron deep – creating an ultra-smooth, hardened surface that virtually eliminates friction and wear.

  • ✅ Permanent bonding – does not wash away with oil changes
  • ✅ Extends oil change intervals by 2 to 3 times
  • ✅ Lowers engine temperature & noise – less friction = less heat
  • ✅ Compatible with all petrol & diesel engines – including 2-stroke and wet clutches (except brake systems)

The result: permanent protection, lasting performance, and verified fuel savings.
